The world is loud on the surface, but underneath the noise, there is a signal calling you home.

People are scared.
People are angry.

And everyone thinks they need to pick a fight.

But fights fracture.
Centers transmit.

This moment is not asking you to react.

You do not have to fix the world.
You only have to stop amplifying the frequency that is fracturing it.
